  Engine-free InputBindings + InputContextStack; pause, options and overlays push the menu context. No key codes outside the Cocos adapter.
  HUD hotkey labels come from the bindings.
- configs/ui/{widgets,menu,hud,editor,splash}.json hold the UI sizes, spacing, fonts, colors, times, text-size factors and HUD anchors
  (UiStyle manager, engine-free structs in viewmodel/). A missing key keeps its default and is logged.
- Scene fades keep the design offset (no jump to the left on wide windows); the splash shows at least 1.5 s (min_show_time).
- Level tests run in fixed 1/60 s steps: same result at any frame rate (the brick-wall case used to depend on frame timing).
