Map scale replaces the window-size setting.
- Profile: map_scale (0 = auto, default; 100/150/200). The windowed window is always auto-sized.
- New model::MapCamera: scale from percent, map centred or following the player and stopped at the map edges.
- GameScene scales the playfield and clips it above the player panel when zoomed.
- Settings button "Map scale" (7 languages) replaces "Window size".
- Tests: MapCamera unit test, settings cycle, in-level scale check (menu tests 76, unit 242).
