Fixed garbled menu text after an SD/HD texture switch (and always in the Test build, whose unit tests switch SD/HD).
Cause: TTF font atlases are rasterized at the content scale but cached by font and size only, so after the scale changed
labels reused atlases built for the old scale; the engine purge even hands live labels their old atlas back.
Fix: ApplyHdTextures purges FontAtlasCache and sends ContentScaleChangedEvent; labels made by widgets::CreateLabel take a fresh atlas.
New unit test switches SD -> HD -> SD and checks the label atlas is the cached one, rasterized at the current scale, and the label keeps its size.
