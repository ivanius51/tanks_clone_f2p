Enemy drops and pickups (Phase 2).
- model::DropRoller: weighted drop tables with a pity counter (chance grows after each miss, resets on a drop), seeded random passed in.
- configs/drops.json tables common / rich / boss; enemies name theirs with drop_table.
- New pickups: small repair, small ammo, coin pack. Dropped pickups blink and vanish after their lifetime.
- Difficulty gets a drop chance multiplier.
Playtest: the campaign bot run gave 5 drops in 24 kills (about 21%). The showcase now has drops.png (boss level, enemies destroyed, a dropped pickup). The coin pack looks like the ammo pickup: it needs its own art.
